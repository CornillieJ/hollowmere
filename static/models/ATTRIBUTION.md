The characters (the player, the villagers and the Hollow) are built in code in
`src/scripts/content/folk.ts`: no CC0 set of bipedal animal folk exists.

## folk/

The farmer, Barnaby, Wren, Fen, Mother Hollis and Pike are original work for this game,
generated by `tools/blender/build_folk.py`. No third-party assets.

The Hollow and Pell (`folk/hollow-*.glb`, `folk/pell.glb`) and the props listed in
`tools/blender/build_props.py` (hearth, workbench, chest, mailbox, postcrate, stall, lectern,
chapel, shrine, scarecrow, crate, woodpile, trough, stone, mushrooms, shed, the 4 crops and the
3 held tools) are original work for this game as well.
